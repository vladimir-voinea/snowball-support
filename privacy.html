Privacy policy

Data Not Collected

Snowball does not collect any data about you or how you play.

Home Support Privacy

Effective 27 October 2026

This policy covers the Snowball app, published by Parallax Technologies SRL ("we").

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no account and no sign-in, and it contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. We do not run a server for the game, so nothing you do in it is sent to us.

What stays on your device

Your snowflakes, upgrades, unlocked towns, best runs and settings are stored in the app's own storage on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Motion sensors

Tilt controls read your device's motion sensors while you play, only to steer the ball. These readings are used on the device in real time and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Sharing

If you tap Share my ball, the game hands a picture of your run to the iOS share sheet, and you choose where it goes. We never see it.

Purchases

Snowball is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions are welcome at parallax.tech.ro@gmail.com.
